About Department

Department at a Glance

The department established in 2021 aims to produce graduates with the necessary and advanced knowledge in communication, preparing them to be the leaders in the field of mass communication with morality, creativity, insightful attitudes particularly language skills. The students are introduced to a variety of forms and processes that shape the media landscape: television, radio, cinema, print, podcasts and digital media. The programme has a holistic approach with special emphasis on the practical aspects of the field- Design & Graphics, Computer application, Newspaper Designing, Photography, Videography, Television and Audio Production, Event Management, Documentary Making, Internship, Event Management.

HOD's Message

Journalism is not just a profession; it’s a powerful tool for shaping public opinion, fostering transparency, and driving social change. As the Head of the Department of Journalism at National Post Graduate College, I am committed to nurturing the next generation of media professionals who will uphold the highest standards of integrity, creativity, and critical thinking in their work. Our department offers a dynamic curriculum that combines theoretical knowledge with practical skills, preparing our students to excel in the rapidly evolving media landscape. We encourage our students to be curious, ethical, and innovative storytellers who can make a positive impact on society through their work in journalism.

Expert Mentors

Dr. Bushra Tufail

Assistant Professor Ph.D.

Dr. Bushra Tufail is an accomplished media professional, educator, and has been a Radio Jockey with 22 years of experience in broadcasting, anchoring, and teaching. She has served as an Assistant Professor at Shri Ramswaroop Memorial University and National PG College, where she taught subjects such as Corporate Communication, Media Management, Radio Journalism, and Business Communication. She has been associated with All India Radio’s FM (Rainbow) channel since 2000 as a Radio Jockey—scripting and presenting shows, interviewing notable personalities, and hosting quiz and music-based programs. One of her radio promos, “Anarkali,” was adjudged Best Promo at the International Radio Festival in Iran (2009). Bushra has also contributed extensively to social causes. She recorded audiobooks, novels, and academic content for the Rehabilitation Society for the Visually Impaired (RSVI)—including classic works in English, Hindi, and Urdu that are accessed by visually impaired students worldwide. She has judged debates and conducted stage programs during Lucknow Mahotsava and events organized by The Hindustan Times. As a faculty member, she has played key roles in NAAC-related documentation, coordinated literary and anchoring clubs, and conducted workshops on communication, GD preparation, and personality development. She has presented research papers in national and international seminars covering media studies, advertising, cinema, and literacy awareness. Currently serving as Head, Department of Journalism & Mass Communication, National PG College, Lucknow Dr. Bushra Tufail continues to blend academics and industry practice with passion. She is actively engaged with the students of mass communication in producing documentaries, organising events as a part of their curriculum.

Ms. Kirti Srivastava

Assistant Professor MJMC

Ms. Kirti has completed her graduation from University of Lucknow, followed by a Master’s degree in Journalism and Mass Communication and an M.Phil. from Babasaheb Bhimrao Ambedkar University, Lucknow. She qualified the UGC-NET examination in 2018 in the same discipline. She is currently pursuing a Ph.D. from Banaras Hindu University. Alongside her doctoral research, she serves as a full-time faculty member in the Department of Journalism and Mass Communication at National Post Graduate College. She has published several research papers in reputed journals and contributed book chapters to edited volumes in her area of expertise. In recognition of her academic achievements, she was awarded the ICSSR Doctoral Fellowship in 2021 by the Indian Council of Social Science Research. She also brings valuable industry experience, having worked with organizations such as Hindustan Times and Kaivalya Communication, among others.
