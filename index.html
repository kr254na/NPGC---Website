Institutional Profile

A Noble Legacy of Educational Excellence

National Post Graduate College, popularly known as NPGC, is one of the most prestigious educational institutions in Lucknow. Founded in 1974, the college has been a beacon of learning for over five decades.

Our college offers a wide range of undergraduate and postgraduate programs across Arts, Science, Commerce, and vocational faculties. We are committed to developing well-rounded, future-ready individuals through innovative teaching, disciplined character-building, and values-based education.
