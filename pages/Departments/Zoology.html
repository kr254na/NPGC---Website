About Department

Department at a Glance

The dynamic and forward-thinking Department of Zoology is dedicated to imparting high-quality education in animal sciences and related biological fields. The department has been committed to helping students enhance their scientific knowledge, practical skills, environmental awareness, and research aptitude since its founding in the year 2005. With a focus on both theoretical knowledge and hands-on experience in the areas of taxonomy, physiology, genetics, ecology, wildlife biology, and biodiversity conservation, the department provides undergraduate education in Zoology. The department consistently works to support students' intellectual and professional development through cutting-edge teaching-learning techniques, laboratory training, field studies, seminars, and academic events. The vision of the department is to become a centre of excellence in Zoological education by promoting scientific temperament, innovation, ethical values, and environmental responsibility among students. The mission of the department is to provide quality and value-based education, encourage research-oriented learning, develop practical and analytical skills, and create awareness regarding biodiversity conservation and sustainable development. The department also aims to prepare students for higher education, research, entrepreneurship, and various career opportunities in life sciences and allied fields.
The department has well-kept lab with models, charts, prepared slides, preserved specimens, light microscopes, biological tools (pH-meter, DNA isolation unit, Kymograph, Chromatograph, Microtome, BOD-incubator, Digital Balance, Colorimeter, Hemocytometer, Hemoglobinometer etc.) and other teaching-learning materials required for efficient hands-on training. Students' academic and research endeavors are aided by a departmental museum and library. Digital presentations and audiovisual aids are examples of ICT-enabled teaching strategies that are frequently utilized to improve classroom instruction. To give students chances for experiential learning and holistic development, the department hosts educational tours, field trips, workshops, seminars, quiz competitions, and awareness programs. The faculty members of the department are dedicated to maintaining academic excellence and mentoring students through regular guidance and counseling. The department also promotes participation in co-curricular and extension activities related to wildlife conservation, environmental protection, health awareness, and scientific outreach. With its student-centric approach and commitment towards academic advancement, the Department of Zoology continues to contribute significantly to higher education and the development of scientifically aware and socially responsible citizens.
The Department of Zoology offers a three-year B.Sc. programme with an intake capacity of 120 students. Admission is open to students who have passed 10+2 in Science stream with Biology and secured a minimum of 50% aggregate marks. Established in 2005, the department is committed to providing quality education in animal sciences and allied biological fields through a balanced combination of theoretical learning and practical exposure. The programme focuses on developing scientific knowledge, practical skills, research aptitude, and environmental awareness in areas such as taxonomy, physiology, genetics, ecology, wildlife biology, biodiversity conservation etc. The department has well-equipped laboratory with light microscopes, preserved specimens, prepared slides, charts, and modern biological instruments including pH-meter, DNA isolation unit, Microtome, Colorimeter and BOD-incubator. ICT-enabled teaching, seminars, workshops, educational tours, and field studies enrich the learning experience. Dedicated faculty members provide academic mentoring and career guidance to students. The department also encourages co-curricular, extension, and alumni progression, promoting scientific temperament, social responsibility, and preparedness for higher education and diverse career opportunities in life sciences.

Expert Mentors

Dr. Archana Singh

Assistant Professor M.Sc., Ph.D.

Dr. Archana Singh (Assistant Professor) is an accomplished academician and dedicated researcher [M.Sc., Ph.D., UPCST (JRF & SRF)] in the field of Zoology, with more than 22 years of rich teaching experience in higher education. She holds a Master of Science (M.Sc.) from Kanpur University and Doctor of Philosophy (Ph.D.) in Zoology, and has consistently contributed to both academic excellence and scientific research throughout her professional journey. As part of her doctoral research, she carried out extensive laboratory work at King George's Medical University, Lucknow on the topic “Indeterminate Leprosy – An Immunopathological Study.” She has actively participated in research and academic writing, with numerous papers published in both national and international journals. Her scholarly work reflects her deep interest in zoological sciences, research methodology, and interdisciplinary scientific studies. Dr. Singh is known for her commitment to teaching, conceptual clarity, and student-centered guidance. Over the years, she has played an important role in shaping the academic and personal growth of countless students through effective teaching, motivation, and mentorship. Her classroom approach combines knowledge with encouragement, helping students develop confidence, discipline, and scientific thinking. Dr. Archana Singh continues to inspire learners through her passion for education, dedication to research, and sincere commitment to mentoring young minds. Her professional journey reflects academic excellence, meaningful research, and a lasting contribution to the field of Zoology and student development.

Dr. Gyanendra Kumar

Assistant Professor M.Sc., Ph.D.

Dr. Gyanendra Kumar is serving as Assistant Professor and Head of Zoology Department. He is M.Sc., Ph.D., UGC-Doctoral Fellow (JRF and SRF), CSIR-NET (Life Science) and IIT-GATE (Life Science) qualified. Till the year 2026, he has published 18 research papers, 13 books, 13 chapters in edited books, 30 research paper presentations in seminar/conferences and 3 patents. Gupta National Shree Award. Dr. Kumar is dedicated in serving the society by conveying and spreading the knowledge in the field of Science. Through dedication, academic commitment, and active involvement in institutional activities, he works towards nurturing responsible, knowledgeable, and socially aware students capable of contributing meaningfully to society and scientific advancement.
