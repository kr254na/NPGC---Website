About Department

Department at a Glance

The Department of Mathematics is one of the fundamental academic departments of the institution, committed to providing quality education at the undergraduate level. Since 2005, its establishment, the department has been dedicated to developing analytical thinking, problem-solving ability, and logical reasoning among students. It is committed to fostering a deep understanding of mathematical theories and their practical applications in the modern world. The Vision and mission of the department is to become a centre of excellence in Mathematics education and to provide strong foundational knowledge in pure and applied mathematics. Also encouraging students participation in seminars, workshops, and academic activities.
Programs:
Department offering three year courses at undergraduate levels (B.A. & B.Sc. both). The curriculum is designed to align with National Education Policy (NEP-2020) standards.
Faculty:
Mentorship is provided by a team of highly qualified academicians specialized in diverse fields such as Pure and Applied Mathematics.
Infrastructure & Facilities:
Use of modern teaching methods including ICT-enabled classrooms, equipped with interactive panel.
Mathematical Computing Lab: Students are introduced to modern computational tools and software like C++, MATLAB, Mathematica, LaTeX to handle complex modelling tasks and mathematics learning.
Resource Library:
The department has a well-maintained library to support UG students. Department provides the collection of textbooks and reference books in pure and applied mathematics, Availability of journals and research materials, Access to previous year question papers and study materials.
Career Pathways:
Graduates from the Department of Mathematics consistently excel in various sectors, including Higher Education & Research (Pursuing Ph.D. and research fellowships), Technical Sectors (Data Science, Actuarial Science, and Information Technology), Competitive Exams (CSIR-NET, GATE, and civil services)
The department views Mathematics not just as a subject of study, but as a universal language that empowers students to decode the complexities of the universe.

Dr. Nirmal Srivastava

Assistant Professor Ph.D.

Dr. Nirmal Srivastava is an Assistant Professor of Mathematics, associated with National P.G. College, Lucknow. He possesses extensive experience in higher education, research in fluid mechanics, and the application of mathematical sciences. He awarded Ph.D. (Mathematics) form University of Lucknow, Lucknow in year 2016. He was also associated as research project fellow in UGC Major Research Project during 2011 to 2014. He has over a decade of teaching and research experience. He has published 16 research papers in national and international journals, focusing in the field of Fluid Mechanics, Graph Theory and Mathematical Functions. Additionally, he has presented his work at numerous national and international seminars and conferences, including lectures on the Applications of Fluid Mechanics in Real Life. He is Life member of Anusandhan: Vigyaan Shodh Patrika, International Scientific Journal and Bharta Ganita Parishad.
